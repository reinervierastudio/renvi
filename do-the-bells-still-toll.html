Do the Bells Still Toll?

2022 · Oil on canvas · 43 × 35 in

Do the Bells Still Toll? reflects on memory, time, and the persistence of meanings inherited from the past. The question posed by the title transforms the image into a space of uncertainty, inviting the viewer to consider what remains, what has changed, and whether the echoes of earlier experiences continue to resonate in the present.
