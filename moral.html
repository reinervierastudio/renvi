Moral

2016 · Oil on canvas · 32 × 48 in

Moral explores the fragile territory between innocence, judgment, and the values transmitted from one generation to another. Through its symbolic figures and unexpected relationships, the painting invites reflection on how human behavior is observed, interpreted, and ultimately transformed into lessons and memory.
