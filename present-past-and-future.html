Present, Past and Future

2026 · Oil on canvas · 32 × 48 in

Present, Past and Future explores the relationship between time, identity, and human behavior through a symbolic narrative. The fox, facing the viewer, represents the present. The encounter between the eagle and the animal figure in the pond, observed by a woman, symbolizes the past. An Indigenous figure evokes the future. Through these interconnected scenes, the painting reflects on memory, transformation, and the complex relationship between human nature and the passage of time.
